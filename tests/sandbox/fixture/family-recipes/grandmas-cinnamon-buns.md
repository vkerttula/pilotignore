# Grandma's Cinnamon Buns

## Ingredients

- 5 dl milk
- 50 g fresh yeast
- 2 dl sugar
- 2 tsp ground cardamom
- 150 g butter
- about 14 dl flour
- filling: butter, sugar, lots of cinnamon

## Steps

1. Warm the milk and dissolve the yeast in it.
2. Mix in sugar, cardamom and flour; knead in the soft butter.
3. Let rise for 45 minutes, roll out and spread the filling.
4. Roll up, slice, let rise again and bake at 225 °C for 10 minutes.
