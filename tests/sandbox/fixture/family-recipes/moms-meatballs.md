# Mom's Meatballs

## Ingredients

- 400 g minced meat
- 1 dl breadcrumbs
- 1 dl cream
- 1 egg
- 1 small onion, grated
- salt, white pepper, a little allspice

## Steps

1. Soak the breadcrumbs in cream for 10 minutes.
2. Mix everything together and roll into small balls.
3. Fry in butter until browned and cooked through.
