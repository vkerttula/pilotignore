# Tomato Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 cans crushed tomatoes
- 5 dl vegetable stock
- 1 dl cream
- olive oil, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil.
2. Add tomatoes and stock, simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Blend until smooth and stir in the cream.
4. Season with salt and pepper.
