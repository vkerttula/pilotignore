# Fluffy Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 2 dl flour
- 2 dl milk
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Whisk the dry ingredients together.
2. Add milk and egg, whisk until smooth.
3. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
4. Fry on a buttered pan until golden on both sides.
